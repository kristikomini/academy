Disaster, replay and the day you must reprocess

The ability to replay is the reason to choose a log over a queue, and almost nobody rehearses it. Being the person who has is a differentiator.

From the advert

Not in any advert. And the payoff for everything in Part 1: a log you never actually replay is a queue with extra disk.

Replay as a normal operation

🧒 Simple

A bug in a consumer means the data it produced is wrong. With a log you can fix the code, move the position back, and process it all again.

That is only true if you planned for it.

🎓 Professional

Mechanically it is chapter 12: stop the group, --reset-offsets to a timestamp or to the earliest offset, restart. What makes it safe is design: the consumer must be idempotent (chapter 16) or reprocessing doubles every side effect, and retention (chapter 22) must reach back far enough to cover the period you need.

Prefer replaying into a new output topic or a new consumer group rather than overwriting in place. Build the corrected projection alongside the broken one, compare them, then switch — which turns an irreversible operation into a reversible one, and is the version of this answer that sounds like experience.

⚠ Trap

Replay re-triggers every side effect the consumer performs. Reprocessing a month of OrderPlaced events through a consumer that sends confirmation emails sends a month of emails. Before any replay, enumerate the consumer’s external effects and disable or gate the ones that must not repeat — a step that is obvious afterwards and routinely forgotten beforehand.

Multi-region and MirrorMaker

🧒 Simple

For disaster recovery you copy the data to a second cluster in another location, so you can fail over if the first one is gone.

🎓 Professional

MirrorMaker 2 (built on Connect) replicates topics, configuration and consumer-group offsets between clusters — asynchronously, so a failover loses whatever had not replicated. Two shapes: active–passive, where the second cluster is a warm standby, and active–active, where both take writes and each mirrors the other, with topic prefixing to prevent cycles.

The hard part is not replication but offset translation: a given record has a different offset on the target cluster, so a consumer failing over cannot simply resume at the same number. MirrorMaker 2 maintains a mapping to handle this; whether your consumers use it correctly is the thing to test, and the thing nobody tests. Confluent’s Cluster Linking preserves offsets directly and is the commercial answer to the same problem.

Rehearsing it

🧒 Simple

A recovery procedure nobody has ever run is a document, not a capability.

🎓 Professional

Define RPO (how much data you can lose) and RTO (how long you can be down) explicitly, because they determine the architecture rather than following from it — asynchronous replication makes an RPO of zero impossible, and saying so early avoids designing toward a promise you cannot keep.

Then rehearse: replay a topic into a scratch consumer group in a lower environment, time it, and write down what broke. That number — “a full replay of this topic takes forty minutes” — is worth more in an incident than any runbook, and it is the kind of concrete detail that makes an interview answer credible.

Golden rules
  • Replay is only safe if consumers are idempotent and retention reaches back far enough.
  • Replay into a new topic or group, compare, then switch.
  • Replay re-fires every side effect. Enumerate and gate them first.
  • MirrorMaker 2 replicates asynchronously. RPO of zero is not on offer.
  • Offset translation is the hard part of failover, and the untested part.
  • Rehearse and time it. An untested procedure is a document.

“Avete scoperto un bug nel consumer che ha prodotto dati sbagliati per un mese. Cosa fate?”

“È esattamente il caso per cui si sceglie un log invece di una coda: correggo il codice e rielaboro dall’inizio del periodo. Ma prima due controlli. Uno: la retention copre un mese? Se no, non ho più i dati e il discorso finisce lì. Due: quali effetti collaterali ha quel consumer? Se manda email, un replay di un mese manda un mese di email — quindi li disabilito o li isolo prima. Poi rielaboro con un nuovo consumer group verso un topic nuovo, così costruisco la proiezione corretta accanto a quella sbagliata, le confronto e solo dopo commuto: rende l’operazione reversibile.”
