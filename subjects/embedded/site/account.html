Your account, your sync and your data, in one place. The Italian name is on purpose: area riservata is the sign on the door of the signed-in half of every Italian site you are about to meet — INPS, Agenzia delle Entrate, and every gestionale you are ever asked to maintain. It is worth recognising before an interviewer says it.

🧒 Two copies of the same homework book

You did page four at home and page seven at your grandmother's, in two different copies of the same book. Now you want one book. The wrong way is to keep the newer copy and throw the other away — that loses a whole page of work for no reason.

So instead you go through it page by page and keep the better version of each: the higher mark, the longer answer, every drawing from either copy. That is exactly what happens every time this site syncs, and it is why answering questions on a phone can never delete what you did on the laptop.

🎓 The merge, and why last-write-wins is wrong here

Two devices editing the same document is the normal case for a study tool, not the exception. Last-write-wins — the default almost everywhere — would silently discard one of the two, and the loss is unrecoverable: nobody notices a missing month of spaced-repetition history until the cards stop coming back.

So merge() in site/assets/account.js combines the two field by field, and every rule is deliberately the conservative one: the higher XP, the better chapter score, the union of notes and badges, the schedule that has progressed further — but the sum of lapses, because a card you have failed on two machines really is a card you keep failing. Nothing is ever removed by a merge. A stale note is a nuisance; a deleted year of study is not.

On top of that sits ordinary optimistic concurrency: the browser sends back the baseRevision it read, and a write built on a revision the server has since moved past is rejected rather than applied. That is the same conflict two masters have when they retry a write onto the same holding register, and it has the same answer — make the write safe to repeat rather than hoping it arrives exactly once: 58 · Talking to the PLC.

⚠ Read this before you rely on it

Lose the recovery code and the account is gone. There is no mail transport, so there is no reset link and nothing else that could verify you. If you never wrote the code down, issue a new one from this page now, while you are still signed in and it is still free to do.

The leaderboard is not an audit. The server re-derives the figures from your document instead of believing a summary from the browser, which stops the cheapest cheat — but anyone can edit their own local profile and sync it. It is a nudge.

Nothing here is a secret store. The profile is study data — scores, notes, a schedule — and it is treated as study data. Do not paste anything into a note that you would not want sitting in a browser's local storage in plain text, because that is exactly where it sits.
