Gestionali, ERP and fatturazione elettronica

This is the chapter with no English-language equivalent, and it is the one that makes you employable in Modena and Bologna specifically. Electronic invoicing through the SDI is mandatory, the XML format is unforgiving, and rejections arrive asynchronously days later. A developer who understands the flow is immediately useful at any gestionale vendor — and most candidates have never heard of a codice destinatario.

From the advert

"Integrazione con sistemi e servizi di terze parti". In a Modena software house that third-party system is the Agenzia delle Entrate, and the integration is fatturazione elettronica. Zucchetti, TeamSystem and their competitors are headquartered in this region precisely because of this domain.

What a gestionale actually is

🧒 Simple

A gestionale is the software a company runs on: orders, stock, invoices, customers, accounting. It is unglamorous, it is business-critical, and it has usually been in service for fifteen years.

🎓 Professional

Working on one is a distinct discipline, and the differences from a greenfield web project are the things to be ready for:

  • The domain is the hard part, not the technology. Nothing about a CRUD screen is difficult; knowing that a DDT (documento di trasporto) is not an invoice, that a nota di credito is how you correct one, and that stock moves on the DDT while revenue is recognised on the invoice — that is the difficult part, and it is learned by asking.
  • Data outlives everything. Fiscal records must be retained for ten years and be reproducible exactly as issued. That constrains schema changes forever (ch. 29).
  • You are rarely the only writer. A desktop client, a warehouse terminal and a web portal may all write the same database, which is the real reason for stored procedures and triggers (ch. 10b).
  • Users are experts. Someone who has issued invoices for twenty years will tell you exactly what is wrong, in domain language. Listen literally — they are usually right about the rule and wrong about the implementation.
  • Correctness beats elegance. A shop losing a cart is annoying; an invoice with the wrong VAT is a fiscal problem with a deadline attached.

The SDI: how an invoice actually travels

🧒 Simple

You do not send an invoice to your customer. You send an XML file to a government exchange system, which checks it, and either delivers it to the customer or sends it back rejected. The answer does not come immediately.

🎓 Professional

The Sistema di Interscambio, run by the Agenzia delle Entrate, is mandatory for B2B, B2C and public administration invoices between Italian parties. The flow:

  1. You produce a FatturaPA XML file with a prescribed name.
  2. You transmit it — via a provider’s API, PEC, SdICoop web service or the portal. In practice you almost always use an intermediary (Aruba, Zucchetti, Fatture in Cloud, TeamSystem), because they handle the channel, the signature and the ten-year conservazione sostitutiva.
  3. The SDI validates it and returns a ricevuta, asynchronously — minutes to five days.
  4. The receipt is one of a small set of message types, and your system must handle each: RC delivered, NS rejected (with an error code), MC not delivered (the recipient’s channel failed — the invoice is still valid and goes to their fiscal drawer), NE the public-administration acceptance or refusal, DT deadline passed.

The asynchrony is the design constraint. An invoice is not "sent"; it is in a state machine — da inviare, inviata, consegnata, scartata, accettata — and your UI must show which. Modelling this as a boolean inviata is the mistake that forces a rewrite (ch. 03 has the enum pattern for it).

Two rules that surprise people. A rejected (NS) invoice must be resent within five days keeping the same number and date, so numbering is not simply "next". And an accepted invoice cannot be modified — you issue a nota di credito to reverse it and a new invoice to replace it. Immutability is a legal property here, not a design preference.

⚠ Trap

The CodiceDestinatario and the numbering sequence are where new integrations break. The code is seven characters for a private company’s channel, six for a public administration (from IPA), and 0000000 when the recipient is reached by PEC or is a consumer — in which case PECDestinatario may be required instead. Getting it wrong produces an MC or an NS days later, not an error at submission. And the invoice number must be sequential with no gaps within the year per sectional register: generating it before you know the send will succeed leaves a hole that an accountant will find and ask about. Reserve the number inside the same transaction that creates the invoice, and never with an application-side MAX(numero) + 1, which races (ch. 11).

<?php declare(strict_types=1);

/** Read an SDI receipt. Namespaced XML: DOMXPath, not SimpleXML. */
function leggiRicevuta(string $xml): array
{
    $doc = new DOMDocument();
    // No external entities, no network — untrusted XML is an XXE risk (ch. 14b).
    $doc->loadXML($xml, LIBXML_NONET);

    $xp = new DOMXPath($doc);
    $xp->registerNamespace('t', 'http://www.fatturapa.gov.it/sdi/messaggi/v1.0');

    $esito = $xp->evaluate('string(//t:Esito)');
    $errori = [];
    foreach ($xp->query('//t:ListaErrori/t:Errore') as $e) {
        $errori[] = [
            'codice'      => $xp->evaluate('string(t:Codice)', $e),
            'descrizione' => $xp->evaluate('string(t:Descrizione)', $e),
        ];
    }

    return [
        'identificativo' => $xp->evaluate('string(//t:IdentificativoSdI)'),
        'esito'          => $esito,          // RC · NS · MC · NE · DT
        'errori'         => $errori,         // NS carries the reason — surface it to the user
    ];
}

Integrating with an ERP you cannot change

🧒 Simple

The company’s existing system is the boss. It owns the customers, the prices and the stock, and your web application is a guest. The job is to synchronise without ever being the one that corrupts the master data.

🎓 Professional

Decide who owns each field, and write it down. That single table — field, owner, direction, frequency — prevents most integration disasters. Typically the ERP owns products, prices and stock; the web owns orders and web-only customer data. Two-way synchronisation of the same field is where you end up with a conflict resolution problem nobody scoped.

Expect the interface to be unpleasant: a nightly CSV over FTP, a SOAP endpoint, a direct read-only database view, or an ODBC connection to something ancient (ch. 14b). Zucchetti and TeamSystem do publish real APIs, and you may equally be handed a folder.

The mechanics that make it survivable:

  • Idempotent imports keyed on the ERP’s own identifier, so a file delivered twice changes nothing (ch. 14b).
  • Never let a sync delete on the basis of absence. A truncated export file must not empty your catalogue — sanity-check the row count before applying.
  • Reconcile, do not trust. A nightly job that compares counts and totals and reports differences is how you find out the sync has been silently failing since Tuesday (ch. 25).
  • Keep a staging table: import raw, validate, then apply. It gives you the failed rows to show the client instead of an aborted job.
  • Codes are strings. A codice articolo like 0012 becomes 12 the moment something treats it as an integer, and then nothing matches.
Golden rules
  • An invoice is a state machine, not a boolean. The SDI answers asynchronously.
  • Handle NS, MC and NE explicitly, and show the error code to the user.
  • Numbering is sequential with no gaps. Reserve it in the transaction, never MAX+1.
  • An accepted invoice is immutable. Correct with a nota di credito.
  • CodiceDestinatario: 7, 6, or 0000000. Wrong means a rejection days later.
  • Write down who owns each field. Two-way sync on one field is a trap.
  • Never delete on absence from an import file.
  • Codes are strings. Leading zeros are meaningful.

"Ha mai lavorato con la fatturazione elettronica?"

Even if the answer is "only studied it", show that you understand the shape, which is what they are testing: "So come funziona il flusso: si genera un XML FatturaPA e lo si trasmette allo SDI, di solito tramite un intermediario che gestisce canale, firma e conservazione. La cosa che cambia il modo di progettare è che la risposta è asincrona: la fattura non è 'inviata', è in uno stato — inviata, consegnata, scartata, accettata — e le ricevute possono arrivare anche dopo giorni. Quindi la modello come macchina a stati e gestisco esplicitamente NS, MC e NE, mostrando all’utente il codice di errore, perché una fattura scartata va corretta e rinviata entro cinque giorni mantenendo numero e data. E la numerazione dev’essere progressiva senza buchi, quindi il numero lo assegno nella stessa transazione e non con un MAX+1 che va in concorrenza."
