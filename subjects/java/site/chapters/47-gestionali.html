Gestionali, ERP integrations and the Italian software house

What the work is actually like behind these adverts: business-management applications, ERP exports, electronic invoicing, and a client whose real specification is a spreadsheet. Knowing the vocabulary before the interview is worth more than another framework.

From the advert

Not in any advert — because it is the shape of the job rather than a skill. It is also the majority of what these companies actually build.

What a gestionale is

🧒 Simple

A gestionale is a business-management application: orders, stock, invoices, production. Forms over data, with decades of accumulated rules.

If a recruiter says gestionale, they are describing the job.

🎓 Professional

The technical profile is distinctive: a large and irregular domain model, heavy reporting, a long-lived schema, integrations in several directions, and business rules that are genuinely complicated rather than accidentally so. The hard part is rarely the technology — it is that fattura, bolla, DDT and commessa each mean something precise, and getting one wrong produces a legal problem rather than a bug report.

Vocabulary worth having before the first call: anagrafica (master data), commessa (a job or project order), DDT (delivery note), fattura (invoice), partita IVA (VAT number), collaudo (acceptance testing), presa in carico (taking ownership of a ticket), avviamento (go-live).

Fatturazione elettronica and SDI

🧒 Simple

In Italy, business invoices are not PDFs. They are XML files sent through a government system called SDI, which validates them and can reject them.

🎓 Professional

Fatturazione elettronica has been mandatory for B2B since 2019: invoices are FatturaPA XML, digitally signed, transmitted through the Sistema di Interscambio, which returns receipts — delivered, not delivered, or rejected with an error code. Rejections must be corrected and resubmitted within a deadline, and everything must be archived under conservazione sostitutiva rules for ten years.

Why it matters technically: this is an asynchronous integration with a stateful workflow and legal deadlines — exactly the sort of thing chapter 33’s messaging and chapter 17’s idempotency exist for. Most companies use an intermediary rather than talking to SDI directly. Knowing what SDI is and that receipts arrive asynchronously puts you ahead of nearly every candidate who has not worked in Italy.

⚠ Trap

Treating invoice numbering as an application concern is a genuine legal problem, not a design preference: numbering must be sequential without gaps per year and per sezionale. A @GeneratedValue sequence with gaps (chapter 27) is not acceptable, and neither is a number allocated before the invoice is certain to exist.

How the work runs

🧒 Simple

Much of it is project work for a client: fixed scope, fixed date, and a specification that turns out to be incomplete. The skill is managing that, not just coding.

🎓 Professional

Expect commesse (fixed-scope client projects) alongside product work, direct contact with the client’s domain expert, and requirements that arrive as a spreadsheet. Expect to integrate with SAP, an existing gestionale, or a machine (chapter 34) via CSV, SFTP or a database view — because that is what the other side supports, whatever you would prefer.

The people who do well are the ones who ask domain questions early and write down the answers. In an interview, showing curiosity about the domain rather than only about the stack is a differentiator: these companies have met plenty of developers who wanted to talk about frameworks and few who wanted to understand what a DDT is for.

Golden rules
  • Gestionale describes the job. Learn the domain vocabulary before the first call.
  • Fatturazione elettronica is XML through SDI, asynchronous, with receipts and deadlines.
  • Invoice numbering is a legal constraint, not an application detail.
  • Integrations arrive as CSV, SFTP or a database view. Meet the other side where it is.
  • The spreadsheet is the specification. Ask domain questions early, and write the answers down.
  • Curiosity about the domain is a differentiator. Most candidates only ask about the stack.

“Sa cos’è un gestionale? Ha mai lavorato su integrazioni?”

“Sì: un applicativo di gestione aziendale — anagrafiche, ordini, magazzino, fatturazione — con un dominio ampio e regole stratificate negli anni. La parte difficile di solito non è tecnica ma di dominio: fattura, DDT e commessa hanno significati precisi e sbagliarne uno è un problema legale, non un bug. Sulle integrazioni mi aspetto di incontrare quello che supporta l’altra parte — CSV, SFTP, una vista sul database — e per la fatturazione elettronica il flusso è asincrono verso SDI, con ricevute che tornano dopo, quindi va trattato come un’integrazione con stato e non come una chiamata sincrona.”
